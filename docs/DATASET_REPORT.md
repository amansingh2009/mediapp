# Dataset audit

| Source | Available labels | Use now | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [RxHandBD](https://zenodo.org/records/18478741), MIT | 5,578 labeled prescription word crops | Existing TrOCR training and word recognition testing | No page layout, regimen fields, or writer identities; word accuracy does not establish complete prescription accuracy. |
| [chinmays18 medical-prescription-dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/chinmays18/medical-prescription-dataset) | Three locally downloaded synthetic full pages with annotation text | Smoke and regression testing only | Small, synthetic, and licensing provenance needs further confirmation; excluded from training and clinical claims. |
| [medocr-vision-dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/naazimsnh02/medocr-vision-dataset), listed MIT | Approximately 2.46k image/text examples used by the candidate model | Candidate for manual review, not downloaded | Its public viewer includes unrelated receipts, invoices, and medical documents; some visible text appears identifiable. The dataset name and license alone do not make every image suitable for prescription training or an independent test. |
| [EkaCare medical records parsing validation set](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ekacare/medical_records_parsing_validation_set), MIT | 288 de-identified Indian medical document images with expert rubrics; prescriptions are mixed with lab reports | Independent evaluation candidate, not downloaded | Hugging Face requires account login and acceptance of repository conditions. It is a test-only set (0 train), and medicine-field labels are not exposed publicly. Do not bypass the gate or train on it. |
| [Mendeley bilingual prescriptions](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/tg2hm7n2bs/2), CC BY 4.0 | 1,000 Bangladesh handwritten prescription images with Bangla/English text annotations | External generalization candidate, not downloaded | Source says patient identifiers were blacked out; verify image-level redaction before use. Geography and language differ from the Indian target, and field labels may require careful conversion. |

No locally available dataset currently supplies an independent, clinician-verified set of real Indian full prescriptions with medicine name, strength, dose, frequency, and duration labels. That is the main accuracy blocker. User uploads remain outside training by default. Before training or model promotion, acquire permitted, de-identified examples, verify their transcriptions, and split by prescription and doctor/writer where those IDs are available. Never train on the locked test set.
